## Rental duration and pricing rules

From **Terms and Conditions** (`/refund_returns/`):

- Rentals available **per hour or per day**.
- **A day = maximum 10 hours, not 24 hours.**
- Payment due at booking or at start of rental.
- A non-refundable deposit covers the first 10 hours (amount “as specified in the listing” — not shown as a number on product pages).
- Refunds only if cancelled **at least 24 hours** before start; non-refundable deposit deducted.
- Cancellation under 24 hours: non-refundable.
- No-show: entire fee forfeited.

**FAQ contradicts T&Cs on cancellation:** FAQ says free cancellation up to **48 hours**; fee within 48 hours. Confirm which rule is current.

Product cards show **USD per day** or a **USD range**. Kia Pegas has attributes **Location: In Accra / Outside Accra**. Booking forms use pickup/drop-off **Accra / Other Location** or **Main Office**. The published ranges are therefore best read as **in-Accra vs outside-Accra daily rates**, not weekly/monthly packages. Weekly/monthly rates are **not published**.

GHS equivalents did not render as static HTML (JS currency switcher). Capture GHS in admin/CMS after confirmation; do not invent a conversion.

## Policies and FAQs (real FAQ page)

Documents: valid driver’s licence; passport or national ID; credit/debit card for reservation and security deposit.

Minimum age (FAQ): **25+** with a valid licence. Age gate on shop: **18+** and photo ID at pickup. **Conflict — confirm.**

No hidden fees; total shown on reservation.

Basic insurance included; optional extra cover.

Additional driver allowed if they present licence and ID.

Airport pickup/drop-off: provide flight details.

Extensions: contact customer service; subject to availability.

Use **within Ghana only**; no international borders.

FAQ cancellation: free up to 48 hours (conflicts with T&Cs 24 hours).

T&Cs extras: authorised drivers only; no illegal use, off-road, racing, or towing unless permitted; return in same condition; renter liable for damage/fines; Ghana law and courts.
